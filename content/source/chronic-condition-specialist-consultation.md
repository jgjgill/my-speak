# 1단계: 한글 스크립트

안녕하세요, 저는 오늘 만성 질환 전문의와 상담이 있어요.
네, 예약 확인되었습니다. 어떤 점이 불편하신가요?
저는 **몇 달 동안 계속 피로를 느껴요.**{I have been feeling tired for several months.}
알겠습니다. **이전에 진단받은 만성 질환**{any previously diagnosed chronic conditions}이 있으신가요?
네, **고혈압**{high blood pressure}이 있습니다.
**최근에 증상이 더 심해졌나요?**{Have your symptoms worsened recently?}
네, **점점 더 나빠지는 것 같아요.**{Yes, it seems to be getting worse and worse.}
**그렇다면 몇 가지 검사를 해보고**{In that case, we will do some tests} **가장 적절한 치료법을 찾아봐야겠네요.**{and find the most appropriate treatment.}

# 2단계: 외국어 스크립트

Hello, I have an appointment with the chronic condition specialist today.
Yes, your appointment is confirmed. What are you feeling unwell with?
I have been feeling tired for several months.
Understood. Do you have any previously diagnosed chronic conditions?
Yes, I have high blood pressure.
Have your symptoms worsened recently?
Yes, it seems to be getting worse and worse.
In that case, we will do some tests and find the most appropriate treatment.
