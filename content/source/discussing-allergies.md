# 1단계: 한글 스크립트

안녕하세요, 의사 선생님. 제가 알레르기 증상이 좀 있어서요.
아이고, 그러시군요. 어떤 증상이신가요?
며칠 전부터 **콧물**{runny nose}이 나고 **눈이 가려워요**{itchy eyes}.
혹시 무엇 때문에 심해지는 것 같으신가요?
밖에 나가면 더 심해지는 것 같아서, **아마 꽃가루**{I think it might be pollen} 때문인 것 같아요.
네, 알겠습니다. 우선 **체온이랑 혈압**{temperature and blood pressure}을 재 볼게요.

# 2단계: 외국어 스크립트

Hello, doctor. I've been having some allergy symptoms.
Oh, I'm sorry to hear that. What seems to be the problem?
I've had a runny nose and itchy eyes for a few days.
Have you noticed anything that might be causing it?
I think it might be pollen, as it's worse when I go outside.
Okay, I'll take a look. Let's check your temperature and blood pressure first.
