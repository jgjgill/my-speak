# 1단계: 한글 스크립트

안녕하세요, **새 프로젝트의 타임라인에 대해 논의하고 싶습니다.**{I wanted to discuss the timeline for the new project.}
물론이죠. **어떻게 생각하시나요?**{What are your thoughts?}
제 생각에는 **현재 마감일이 너무 촉박한 것 같습니다.**{I think the current deadline is a bit too aggressive.}
**테스트를 위해 더 많은 시간이 필요할 것 같습니다.**{We might need more time for testing.}
**수정된 타임라인 제안은 무엇인가요?**{What's your proposed revised timeline?}
**최종 납품일을 2주 뒤로 늦추는 것은 어떨까요?**{How about we push the final delivery date back by two weeks?}
**그게 더 관리하기 쉬울 것 같습니다.**{That sounds more manageable.}
**그렇게 합시다.**{Let's go with that.}

# 2단계: 외국어 스크립트

Hello, I wanted to discuss the timeline for the new project.
Sure. What are your thoughts?
I think the current deadline is a bit too aggressive.
We might need more time for testing.
What's your proposed revised timeline?
How about we push the final delivery date back by two weeks?
That sounds more manageable.
Let's go with that.
