# 1단계: 한글 스크립트

오늘 정말 **멋져 보여요**{look great today}!
이 셔츠는 정말 **당신에게 잘 어울려요**{really suits you}.
**머리 스타일이 멋진데요**{Your hairstyle is cool}!
와, **칭찬 감사합니다**{thank you for the compliment}!
**당신은 정말 친절해요**{You are very kind}.
이 음식 **정말 맛있어요**{is really delicious}!
이거 **정말 잘 만들었어요**{You made this really well}.

# 2단계: 외국어 스크립트

You look great today!
This shirt really suits you.
Your hairstyle is cool!
Wow, thank you for the compliment!
You are very kind.
This food is really delicious!
You made this really well.
