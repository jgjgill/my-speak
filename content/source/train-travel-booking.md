# 1단계: 한글 스크립트

안녕하세요, **기차표를 예매하고 싶어요**{I'd like to book a train ticket}.
네, **어디로 가시나요**{Where would you like to go}?
**부산으로 가고 싶어요**{To Busan, please}.
**언제 여행하시겠어요**{And when would you like to travel}?
**내일 아침이요**{Tomorrow morning}.
네, **내일 아침 부산행 표 한 장**{One ticket to Busan for tomorrow morning}이고, **50,000원**{50,000 won}입니다.
감사합니다.

# 2단계: 외국어 스크립트

Hello, I'd like to book a train ticket.
Okay. Where would you like to go?
To Busan, please.
And when would you like to travel?
Tomorrow morning.
Okay. One ticket to Busan for tomorrow morning, and that will be 50,000 won.
Thank you.
