# 1단계: 한글 스크립트

주말에 낚시 갈 생각인데, 시간 괜찮아?
오, 정말 재밌겠다! **어디로 갈지 생각해 봤어?**{Where were you thinking of going?}
공원 근처 호수로 갈까 생각 중이야. **낚시 장비 가지고 있어?**{Do you have any fishing gear?}
응, 낚싯대랑 릴 있어. **몇 시에 만날까?**{What time should we meet?}
오전 9시쯤 어때? **간식도 좀 챙겨야 할 것 같아.**{We should also bring some snacks.}
좋아! 샌드위치 좀 싸갈게. **정말 기대돼.**{I'm really looking forward to it.}

# 2단계: 외국어 스크립트

I was thinking about going fishing this weekend, are you free?
Oh, that sounds fun! Where were you thinking of going?
I was considering the lake near the park. Do you have any fishing gear?
Yes, I have a rod and reel. What time should we meet?
How about around 9 AM? We should also bring some snacks.
Great! I'll pack some sandwiches. I'm really looking forward to it.
