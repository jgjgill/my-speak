# 1단계: 한글 스크립트

```korean
최근 시장 동향에 대해 이야기 좀 해볼까요?
좋아요. **특히 어떤 점**{what specifically}이 궁금하신가요?
이번 분기 **판매량이 크게 늘었다**{sales have increased significantly}는 보고서를 봤어요. **이게 정말 놀랍네요.**{This is really surprising.}
정말요? **그 이유가 뭐라고 생각하세요**{What do you think is the reason for that}?
아마 **새로운 마케팅 캠페인**{new marketing campaign} 덕분인 것 같아요. **그것 때문에 더 많은 고객들이 관심을 보였어요.**{Because of that, more customers showed interest.}
맞아요. **경쟁사들이 주춤하고 있는 것**{competitors are slowing down}도 한몫한 것 같고요. **전반적으로 상황이 좋습니다.**{Overall, the situation is good.}
네, **더 자세히 알아봐야겠어요.**{We should look into it further.}

# 2단계: 외국어 스크립트

```english
Let's talk about recent market trends.
Sure. What specifically are you interested in?
I read that sales have increased significantly this quarter. This is very surprising.
Really? What do you think is the reason for that?
Maybe it's because of the new marketing campaign; it attracted more customers.
I agree. Also, competitors are slowing down. The overall market looks positive.
Yes, we should investigate this further.
