# 1단계: 한글 스크립트

안녕하세요, 예약한 김민준입니다.
네, 김민준님. 어디가 불편해서 오셨나요?
**머리가 아픈데요**{I have a headache}, **3일째 계속되고 있어요**{it's been three days}.
두통이 **언제부터**{since when} 시작되었고, **어떤 종류**{what kind}의 통증인가요?
**찌르는 듯한**{stabbing} 통증이고, **주로 이마 쪽**{mostly on the forehead}이 아파요.
**메스꺼움**{nausea}이나 **어지럼증**{dizziness}은 없으신가요?
아니요, **그런 증상은 없어요**{I don't have those symptoms}.
알겠습니다. 좀 더 자세히 진찰해 볼게요.

# 2단계: 외국어 스크립트

Hello, I'm Kim Minjun, I have an appointment.
Yes, Mr. Kim Minjun. What brings you here today?
I have a headache, it's been three days.
When did the headache start, and what kind of pain is it?
It's a stabbing pain, and it mostly hurts on the forehead.
Do you have any nausea or dizziness?
No, I don't have those symptoms.
Understood. I will examine you more closely.
