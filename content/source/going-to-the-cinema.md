# 1단계: 한글 스크립트

안녕하세요! 무엇을 도와드릴까요?
저는 **영화표 두 장**{two movie tickets}을 사고 싶어요.
네, 어떤 영화로 드릴까요?
**가장 인기 있는 영화**{the most popular movie}로 주세요.
**오늘 저녁 7시 상영**{the 7 PM showing tonight}으로 드릴까요?
네, 좋아요. **결제는 어떻게 하시겠어요?**{How would you like to pay?}
**카드로 결제할게요.**{I'll pay by card.}

# 2단계: 외국어 스크립트

Hello! How can I help you?
I want to buy two movie tickets.
Okay, which movie would you like?
Please give me the most popular movie.
Would you like the 7 PM showing tonight?
Yes, that sounds good. How would you like to pay?
I'll pay by card.
