# 1단계: 한글 스크립트

안녕하세요, 최근 저희 **투자 포트폴리오 성과**{investment portfolio performance}는 어떤가요?
사실 꽤 좋아요! 우리가 투자한 **주식들이 성장했어요**{stocks have grown} (약 15% 정도).
채권은 어떤가요?
채권은 **안정적이에요**{is stable} (3% 정도의 **완만한 상승세**{modest gain}).
전반적으로 포트폴리오가 **잘 운영되고 있어요**{performing well}.
그렇게 들으니 기쁘네요. 다음 분기에 **다시 검토해 봐야겠어요**{should review it again}.

# 2단계: 외국어 스크립트

Hello, how has our investment portfolio performance been lately?
Actually, it's quite good! The stocks we invested in have grown (by about 15%).
What about the bonds?
The bonds are stable (showing a modest gain of about 3%). Overall, the portfolio is performing well.
I'm glad to hear that. We should review it again next quarter.
